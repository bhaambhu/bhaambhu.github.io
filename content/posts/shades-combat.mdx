**SHADES: Combat** is a 3D top-down shooter game that has over 1.5 million players. The game includes real-time multiplayer, and in this post, I’ll share the challenges and solutions involved in building such a system.

1. **Concurrency Control**  
   Managing multiple players in real-time, both in peer-to-peer LAN and online multiplayer environments.

2. **AI and Game Design**  
   Developing intelligent enemy AI that adapts to different levels of player interaction.

3. **In-App Purchases and Ads**  
   Integrating Google Play Services for monetization via ads and in-app purchases.

This project was a huge learning curve in multiplayer systems. View it on [GitHub](https://github.com/bhaambhu/shades).
